Frequently Asked Questions

Click on a question to reveal the answer.

What is your shipping policy?

We offer free shipping on all orders over $50. Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days, and express shipping (2-3 business days) is available for an additional fee. All orders are processed within 24 hours.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. Products must be unused and in their original packaging. To initiate a return, please contact our support team with your order number.

Are your products cruelty-free?

Yes! All Glow Haven products are 100% cruelty-free. We never test on animals, and we are certified by leading cruelty-free organizations. Our products are also vegan-friendly.

How do I know which products are right for my skin?

We recommend taking our Skin Quiz! It asks 5 quick questions about your skin type, concerns, and goals, and then recommends products tailored just for you. You can also consult our Skin Guide for more information.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to over 45 countries worldwide. International shipping times vary by location, typically 7-14 business days. Duties and taxes may apply and are calculated at checkout.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All transactions are secure and encrypted for your protection.

Can I change or cancel my order?

You can cancel or modify your order within 2 hours of placing it. Please contact our support team immediately with your order number and we'll do our best to accommodate your request.
